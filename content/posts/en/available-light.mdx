title: Available light
excerpt: Flash can save an assignment. Available light tells you where you actually stood.
date: "2026-08-18"

---

I like a window more than a kit. A doorway, a bus shelter, a lamp that was already there before I arrived.

Available light forces you to move your feet. If the face is dead, you take one step, not one stop. If the background burns, you wait for a cloud or you turn the person until the highlight sits on the cheekbone.

On deadline that luxury disappears. Then you add light and you own the lie. The honest frame, when you can afford it, is the one that uses the room as it was.
