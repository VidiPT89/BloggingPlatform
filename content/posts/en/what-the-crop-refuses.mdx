I used to think the crop was a rescue. A way to save a sloppy file. Sixteen years of assignments taught me the opposite.

You crop to protect the subject. A hand at the edge can steal the whole frame. A logo on a wall can turn a portrait into an advert. A sky that is only weather can flatten a face.

When I edit a sequence, I ask one question: what can leave without the story falling.

The photograph that stays is the one that still works after you have been unkind to it.
