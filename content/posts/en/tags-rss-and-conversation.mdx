A journal without a way out is a drawer. RSS is the door. Each note gets a title, a link, a date and a category so a reader can subscribe without creating an account here.

Open Graph images are the poster on the street. This app paints one per slug: black field, burnt orange rule, the title in a display face.

Comments stay optional. Giscus attaches GitHub Discussions to a post. If the environment variables are empty, the desk stays closed and the note still stands on its own.
