The first useful lesson in a darkroom is not contrast. It is time.

You rock the tray. You wait. If you pull the sheet early, you get a ghost. If you leave it too long, the blacks close and the story drowns.

Street work has the same clock. You do not force a gesture. You stay until the person forgets you are there, and the frame settles on its own.

A good print still smells a little like the stop bath. A good photograph still feels like you almost missed it.
